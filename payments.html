What happens when I run out of credits?
Try-on requests pause until you top up or renew. Top-up packs never expire and carry over indefinitely.
When do plan credits expire?
Plan credits expire at the end of your billing period. Credits bought separately never expire.
Is payment secure?
All payments go through Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). We never store card details. UPI, cards, net banking, and wallets supported.
Can I upgrade or downgrade?
Upgrades take effect immediately. For downgrades or cancellations contact support — we'll sort it out right away.
