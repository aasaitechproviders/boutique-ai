❓Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I run out of credits?
Try-on requests are paused until you top up or renew. Top-up packs never expire and carry over.
When do plan credits expire?
Plan credits expire at the end of your billing period. Credits bought as top-up packs never expire.
Is my payment secure?
Payments are processed by Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). We never store card details. UPI, cards, net banking, and wallets all supported.
Can I upgrade or downgrade?
Upgrades take effect immediately. For downgrades or cancellations, contact support.
